# fastrc: Fast Reverse Complement of DNA and RNA Sequences

Fast reverse complement of DNA and RNA sequences using a 'C++' lookup
table for O(1) per-base complement mapping. Supports full IUPAC
ambiguity codes, DNA and RNA modes, case preservation, and NA handling.
Much faster than other packages for computing reverse complements of
many short sequences such as primers, probes, and, k-mers.
